🛡️ Authority Rules & Regulations

  1. Verify every report. Check the photo, location and description.
  2. Prioritize serious areas. Give priority to rivers, roads, schools and public places.
  3. Update report status.
    Pending → In Progress → Completed
  4. Verify cleanup. Confirm that the plastic waste has been removed before completion.
  5. Protect privacy. Do not publicly display reporter personal information.
  6. Follow safety rules. Cleanup activities should follow applicable local safety guidelines.
  7. Maintain proper records. Keep report and cleanup information updated.
  8. No false reports. Only genuine plastic waste reports should be processed.
♻️ Plastic Watch Goal

Reduce plastic pollution and create cleaner public places.
