ℹ️ About Plastic Watch

Plastic Watch is a web-based system designed to help people report plastic waste.

Citizens can report plastic waste with location, photo and description.

Authorities can manage the reports and update the cleanup status.

♻️ Our Goal

To reduce plastic pollution and create cleaner public places.
